# Desktop preview video

Copyright © 2026 [nhocconan](https://x.com/nhocconan).

[Download or play the short preview](manga-sub-preview.mp4).

The preview opens a local HTTP fixture in the macOS desktop app, shows the
reader before translation, presses **Translate**, and stops after Vietnamese
text appears over the comic image. It includes the app screenshots, captions,
and a Vietnamese voice track. The fixture uses the included sample artwork and
does not contact a real comic site or provider.
